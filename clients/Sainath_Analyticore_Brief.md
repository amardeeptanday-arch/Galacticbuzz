## Outreach playbook (James's docs are reference, not rules; we run it our way)

**Personas**
- Decision Makers (COO primary; CEO/CFO): open with an operational observation, no pitch, aim for a reply
- Programme Leads (Dir. Ops/Supply Chain/Manufacturing): practitioner to practitioner, Sainath's natural register
- Technical Influencers (Head of Data, Enterprise Architect, Data Platform): engineering credibility, real Foundry terms (ontology, RIDs, Workshop), name the limitations
- End Users (Data Engineers/Analysts): pure peer, never pitch

**Tone rules:** lead with an observation, not a claim. No Analyticore/Palantir mention in the connection request. No stats in DM1. Ask for a reply, not a meeting. Use short, blunt lines. Raise staff aug only in DM3, and only when hiring signals are present.

**Cadence:** Connection → DM1 (day 3) → DM2 (day 8, article share) → DM3 (day 14, soft ask) → InMail (day 21, only with a fresh signal)

**InMail only for:** re-approaching after 2–3 weeks of silence, or very senior contacts (COO/CDO/CIO) at large accounts.

**Warm signals to flag immediately:** profile view after connecting, a reply that asks a question back, a Calendly click with no booking.

**Flyer** goes out after DM3 warmth. The **brochure** is the leave-behind for serious conversations.
