How we check every firm

Before a firm reaches your shortlist, we check four things. You see the results with each introduction.

  • Company and history
    Registration, years trading, team size
  • Client references
    We call two of their current clients
  • Data security
    NDA, access controls, certificates
  • Communication
    A call with the person who’d run your work
