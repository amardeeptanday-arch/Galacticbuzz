FAQ

Questions we get every week.

Is it really free for buyers?

Yes. Vendors pay Galacticbuzz to be part of the network, so you never pay us for a match. You agree pricing directly with the firm you choose.

How do you vet the firms?

A Galacticbuzz analyst checks each firm’s registration and history, calls two of its US clients, reviews its data security, and runs a recorded communication test. You see the scorecard with every shortlist.

Who sees my brief?

Only Galacticbuzz until you approve a shortlist. Then only the firms you choose see your details. We never sell or publish briefs.

What if none of the firms fit?

Tell us why and we’ll send a second shortlist. There’s no limit and no charge.

Can the firm work in our software and on US hours?

Yes. Tools and time-zone overlap are part of the brief, and we only shortlist firms that already work that way.

Who owns the relationship once we sign?

You do. You contract directly with the firm. We stay on hand for the first 90 days if anything goes wrong.

What does Virtual BD cost?

Plans start with a one-off Research Sprint and move to a monthly Outbound Engine. See Pricing for current plans.

Who sends the outreach, you or us?

Either. We can send from a domain set up for you, or from your own team’s inboxes and LinkedIn profiles.

Are meetings guaranteed?

We agree a meeting target in the pilot and report against it every week. policy to confirm

How do I join the matching network?

Apply on the For Vendors page. We verify every firm before it receives introductions.

Which regions do you cover?

The USA and Canada first, then the UK, the EU, Australia and New Zealand, and the Middle East.

Who owns the data and contacts?

You do. Every target list and contact we research for you is yours to keep.

A 14-person CPA firm in Ohio closed tax season two weeks early.

ClientCPA firm
Matched in3 days
Returns handled420
Season closed−14 days

The challenge

The firm had lost two seasonal preparers in January and had 420 individual and small-business returns due by April 15. Hiring locally would take six weeks they didn’t have.

The match

Their brief asked for UltraTax experience, CPA review on every return, and four hours’ overlap with Eastern time. Galacticbuzz sent three verified firms within 48 hours. They interviewed two and chose Clearbooks Global.

The result

  • Six preparers onboarded in 9 days, working inside the firm’s own UltraTax and portal.
  • All 420 returns filed by April 1, with zero e-file rejections.
  • The firm kept the team for year-round bookkeeping.
