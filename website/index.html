How we check every firm

Before a firm reaches your shortlist, we check four things. You see the results with each introduction.

  • Company and history
    Registration, years trading, team size
  • Client references
    We call two of their current clients
  • Data security
    NDA, access controls, certificates
  • Communication
    A call with the person who’d run your work

Good firms deserve good clients.

What we hold ourselves to

Our values.

  • Say no early.

    If a company or a firm doesn’t fit, we say so, with the reason.

  • Show the work.

    Signals, sources and rejection logs, not just a list of names.

  • Both sides win, or nobody does.

    A bad meeting wastes the buyer’s time and our client’s. We only book the ones we’d stand behind.
