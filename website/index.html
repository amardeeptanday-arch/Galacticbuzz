FAQ

Common questions.

Something else? Ask us.

Who sends the outreach?

We can send from a separate domain set up for you, or from your team’s inboxes and LinkedIn profiles. Most clients start with a separate domain.

Do you take the sales calls?

No. We book qualified meetings and brief you on each buyer. Your team runs the call.

How soon do meetings start?

Research takes about two weeks. Outreach starts after that, so first meetings usually come in weeks 3 to 6.

Is there a minimum term?

The research sprint is one-off. Monthly plans start with a 3-month pilot, then run month to month. confirm

How we check every firm

Before a firm reaches your shortlist, we check four things. You see the results with each introduction.

  • Company and history
    Registration, years trading, team size
  • Client references
    We call two of their current clients
  • Data security
    NDA, access controls, certificates
  • Communication
    A call with the person who’d run your work

FAQ

Questions from buyers.

Is it really free?

Yes. Firms pay to be in our network. You agree fees directly with the firm you hire.

Who sees my brief?

Only our team, and then only the firms on your shortlist. We don’t sell or publish briefs.

What if none of the firms fit?

Tell us why and we’ll look again.

Do the firms work on our hours?

You set the overlap you need, and we only suggest firms that can meet it.
